## The short answer

If you want a **capital-city experience** — international flights, imperial landmarks, maxed-out clinic choice — choose **Beijing**. If you want **lower prices, cooler air and a calm, scenic recovery** among karst mountains, choose **Xingyi, Guizhou**. Both deliver the same implant brands and licensed care; they differ in atmosphere, cost and logistics.

This guide breaks down the real differences so you can book with confidence.

## Price: what you actually pay

Both cities are far cheaper than the US, UK or Australia. Within China, Xingyi is the better-value option because local operating costs are lower.

| Treatment | Beijing (USD) | Xingyi (USD) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single implant (fixture + abutment + crown) | $1,000 – $2,500 | $800 – $1,800 | Same brands in both |
| General check-up & cleaning | $60 – $300 | $50 – $200 | Xingyi slightly lower |
| In-office teeth whitening | $180 – $400 | $150 – $300 | Xingyi slightly lower |

> On a full-mouth or All-on-4 plan, the Xingyi saving can reach several thousand dollars — often more than the cost of your flights.

**Beijing** is our flagship destination. The city hosts some of China's most advanced dental hospitals and private clinics, with internationally trained specialists and equipment on par with any global capital. Our Beijing partner offers implantology, cosmetic and general dentistry under one roof, with English-speaking coordinators at every visit.

**Xingyi** is where the Jingzhou Dental Group began — the hometown of our founder. The clinic pairs provincial-leading equipment with the warmth of a community practice. You are treated by a stable local team that has placed thousands of implants, in a setting where everyone knows the clinic by name.

Both clinics are independently licensed medical institutions in China, verified before we list them.

## Travel & recovery: culture vs calm

This is where the two cities feel completely different.

- **Beijing** — 3,000 years of history on your doorstep. The Great Wall (Mutianyu), the Forbidden City, hutong alleyways and Peking duck. Best if you want a bucket-list city trip and don't mind a busier pace during recovery.
- **Xingyi** — a sea of cone-shaped karst peaks (the 5A-rated Wanfenglin), the waterfall-threaded Maling River Gorge, Bouyei ethnic villages and the record-breaking Hua Jiang Canyon Bridge. Cooler air and slow living make it ideal for restful healing.

Both destinations come with a paced 5-day sample itinerary (consultation → treatment → gentle sightseeing → follow-up) so you never overdo it while recovering.

## Who should choose which?

**Choose Beijing if you:** want the widest choice of specialists, prefer a major international hub with direct long-haul flights, or are combining business with treatment.

**Choose Xingyi if you:** want the lowest price for the same quality, prefer a quiet, nature-rich recovery, or are already planning to explore Guizhou's landscapes.
